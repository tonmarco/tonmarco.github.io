Getting to IC2S2

Arrive at the Conference

The central conference venue is the Leonardo Del Vecchio Building (Piazza Angelo Sraffa 13, 20136 Milan, Italy), at the heart of Bocconi University's urban campus. The venue is well connected by public transportation and within easy reach of Milan's city center, the Navigli district, hotels, restaurants and other amenities.

Access from Major International and European Cities

Milan is served by three airports: Milan Linate Airport (LIN), Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP), and Milan Bergamo Airport (BGY). Linate is closest to the Bocconi campus, while Malpensa provides a broad range of international and intercontinental connections. Milan is also easily accessible by train through its main railway stations.

Arrive via Plane

From Milan Linate Airport (LIN)

Milan Linate Airport is the closest airport to Bocconi University. The journey to campus takes approximately 20 minutes by taxi, depending on traffic.

By public transportation, take Metro line M4 toward the city center, get off at Piazza Tricolore, then take tram 9 toward Porta Genova and get off at Via Bocconi.

  • Milan Linate Airport
  • Milan public transportation information
From Milan Malpensa Airport (MXP)

Milan Malpensa is Milan's main international and intercontinental airport. The journey to the Bocconi campus takes approximately 50 minutes by taxi, depending on traffic.

The Malpensa Express connects Airport Terminals 1 and 2 with Milano Centrale, Milano Porta Garibaldi and Milano Cadorna.

From Milano Cadorna, take Metro line M2 toward Abbiategrasso or Assago Milanofiori Forum, get off at Porta Genova, then take tram 9 to Via Bocconi.

Alternatively, airport shuttle buses connect Malpensa with Milano Centrale. From there, take Metro line M3 toward San Donato, get off at Porta Romana, then take tram 9 toward Porta Genova and get off at Via Bocconi.

  • Milan Malpensa Airport
  • Malpensa Express
From Milan Bergamo Airport (BGY)

Milan Bergamo Airport, also known as Orio al Serio Airport, is connected to Milano Centrale by several airport bus services.

From Milano Centrale, take Metro line M3 toward San Donato, get off at Porta Romana, then take tram 9 toward Porta Genova and get off at Via Bocconi.

  • Milan Bergamo Airport
  • Airport bus connections

Arrive via Train

From Milano Centrale Railway Station

Take Metro line M3 toward San Donato and get off at Porta Romana. Continue with tram 9 toward Porta Genova and get off at Via Bocconi. A taxi journey takes approximately 20 minutes, depending on traffic.

From Milano Porta Garibaldi Railway Station

Take Metro line M2 toward Abbiategrasso, get off at Porta Genova, then take tram 9 toward Stazione Centrale and get off at Via Bocconi.

Milano Tibaldi Railway Station

Milano Tibaldi, served by the S9 suburban railway line, is close to the Bocconi campus. Follow the Bocconi signs inside the station, exit toward Via Bazzi, then continue to the campus entrances on Via Castelbarco or Viale Toscana.

Public Transportation in Milan

Milan's public transportation network includes metro lines, trams, buses and urban rail services. Check the official ATM website or app for current routes, schedules, fares and service changes.

Parking

Participants arriving by car can use paid parking facilities near the Bocconi campus:

  • Parking B: Via Röntgen 1
  • Parking C: Via Bocconi 8
  • Parking D: Via Castiglioni 8

Availability and rates may vary, so check current access conditions and prices before travelling. Reserved spaces for people with disabilities are available on nearby public streets and inside paid parking facilities. Since the campus is well served by public transportation, participants are encouraged to use public transport whenever possible.
