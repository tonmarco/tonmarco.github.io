Venue

Milan, Italy

Milan is an open, international and multicultural city, renowned worldwide for fashion and design and recognized as one of Italy's leading financial, economic and business hubs. IC2S2 2027 will be hosted at Bocconi University, whose urban campus is fully integrated into the city and located within walking distance of downtown Milan and the Navigli district. The campus combines historic buildings with innovative contemporary architecture, reflecting Bocconi's international outlook and its commitment to modernity and sustainability. The main conference venue will be the Leonardo Del Vecchio Building, located in Piazza Sraffa 13 at the heart of the Bocconi campus. Conference participants will be well placed to explore Milan's cultural life, restaurants, public spaces and nightlife while remaining closely connected to the conference venue.

Conference Venue

The Leonardo Del Vecchio Building (Piazza Angelo Sraffa 13, 20136 Milan, Italy) is located at the heart of Bocconi University's urban campus. Designed by Ignazio Gardella and informally known as the “Velodromo” because of its distinctive oval shape, it is one of the University's most recognizable teaching buildings and welcomes thousands of students every day. The campus combines historic and contemporary architecture and reflects Bocconi's commitment to education, research and social impact, embodied in the University's motto, “Knowledge That Matters.” Closely integrated into the city of Milan, the campus offers conference participants easy access to the city center and surrounding neighborhoods. For directions and public transportation, see the travel information.
